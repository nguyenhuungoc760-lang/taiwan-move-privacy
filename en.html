Taiwan Move — Privacy Policy

Tiếng Việt · English

Updated: November 2, 2026. Responsible developer: Nguyễn Hữu Ngọc. Contact: Nguyenhuungoc7600@gmail.com.

This policy describes the Taiwan Move Android closed-testing build with TRA, HSR and YouBike. The app is independent and does not represent the transport operators. It does not require an account or process payments.

Location and searches

When you choose to find nearby stations, the app requests Android location permission. Your location is used on your device to calculate distances and rank nearby YouBike stations. The app does not access your location in the background or keep a location history. You can deny permission and search by name. Accuracy depends on your phone and surroundings.

For train searches, the app sends the necessary requests to the operators, such as departure station, arrival station and travel date for HSR, or downloads timetable files for the selected and adjacent dates for TRA. TRA connection suggestions are calculated on the device. Service providers may receive your IP address, request time and technical connection information. Provider-side logging is outside the app's control.

Data stored on your device

Language, appearance, saved stations and journeys, and the TDX request counter are stored in app storage. Search data is cached temporarily during a session to reduce repeated downloads. The app has no developer-operated server for storing your history and does not automatically synchronize saved items to a developer server.

You can remove saved items in the app, change language or appearance, revoke location permission, or clear app data in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes local data through Android's mechanisms. The app is configured to disable automatic Android backup; device transfers performed by the operating system or manufacturer tools are subject to their own policies.

External services

The app connects directly to TRA, Taiwan High Speed Rail and YouBike. When you choose to open Google Maps, the destination or coordinates needed for directions are passed to Google Maps or an external app. You decide whether to open these links; the receiving service has its own policy.

Advertising and libraries

The first closed-testing release build does not display ads. A separate debug build used for testing may display Google's “Test Ad” banner and have a different configuration. The project includes the Google Mobile Ads library; the release configuration disables ad requests and removes the automatic initialization provider and advertising identifier permissions.

If live ads are enabled later, this policy and the data disclosures will be updated before release. According to Google's documentation, an active advertising SDK may process IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Privacy choices and consent controls will be provided where required; the current testing build should not be considered a complete live-advertising implementation.

MRT and bus features are hidden in this build. The developer has not configured a separate user analytics or crash-reporting service. Local Android logs may be read when you explicitly authorize troubleshooting through a computer. If you send an email or a video to report a problem, avoid including unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is handled through the email provider under its own policy.

Optional TDX access

A technical connection setting allows users with an existing account to enter a short-lived token. The token stays in session memory; the app does not store a Client Secret. When this option is used, TDX may receive the related requests. The app does not register accounts, buy plans or make transactions on your behalf.

Security, limitations and changes

The sources used in this build use HTTPS. Availability, source accuracy and the security of all external services cannot be guaranteed absolutely. Train times are published schedules, not confirmation of on-time operation. This policy will be updated when features or data handling change.
